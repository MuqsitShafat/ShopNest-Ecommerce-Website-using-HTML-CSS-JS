How to Choose a WiFi Security Camera for Your Home or Shop in Pakistan

Plug-in or battery? Do you need a memory card? Will it work in load shedding? A simple guide to choosing and setting up a WiFi camera in Pakistan.

By the ShopNest team · Updated 10 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

Choose the V380 WiFi Camera (Rs 3,999) for a fixed spot like a room, gate view or shop counter — it has night vision, motion alerts and two-way audio. Choose the V380 BK-530 (Rs 4,300) if you need to move it around or place it where there's no socket — it has a built-in battery. Both work with the free V380 app.

What can a WiFi camera actually do?

A WiFi camera connects to your home internet and streams live video to your phone — from the office, a relative's house or abroad. You can watch kids coming home from school, check on elderly parents, keep an eye on a maid or a shop counter, and get a notification the moment it sees movement.

Plug-in or battery camera — which is better?

Plug-in cameras like the V380 WiFi Camera HD Wireless IP are best for a permanent spot. They run 24/7, add infrared night vision and two-way audio (you can talk through the camera), and come with a wall-mount kit.

Battery cameras like the V380 BK-530 Mini Battery WiFi Camera run without a cable, so you can put them on a shelf, near a cot or on a counter in seconds — and move them whenever you like. You can still plug it in for 24/7 use.

Do I need a memory card?

Not for live viewing — that works straight away. If you want to save recordings to watch later, add a microSD (TF) card. The V380 WiFi Camera supports cards up to 64GB. A 32GB card is plenty for most homes because the camera overwrites old footage automatically.

Will it work during load shedding?

A camera needs both power and WiFi. During a power cut:

  • The BK-530 keeps running on its own battery.
  • A plug-in camera can run from a power bank such as the G-Golden 30,000mAh using its USB cable.
  • Your WiFi router also needs backup power — many people run it from a small UPS or a router power bank.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install the free V380 app (Android or iPhone).
  2. Power on the camera and connect your phone to your 2.4GHz WiFi (most home routers have this).
  3. Add the camera in the app by scanning its QR code and follow the steps.
  4. Change the default password straight away — this is the single most important privacy step.
  5. Mount it high, pointing at entrances or the area you care about, and avoid facing bright windows.

Frequently asked questions

Does a WiFi camera need internet all the time?
Yes, to view it from your phone. Without internet, it can still record to a memory card if one is installed.
Will it work with 5GHz WiFi?
Use the 2.4GHz network — most routers broadcast both. 2.4GHz also reaches further through walls.
Can I watch the camera from abroad?
Yes. As long as the camera is online, you can watch from anywhere in the world through the V380 app.
Is it legal to use a camera at home or in my shop?
Cameras in your own home or shop are common. Avoid pointing them into neighbours' private spaces and let staff know if they're being recorded.
