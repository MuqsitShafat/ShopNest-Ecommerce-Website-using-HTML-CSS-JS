Shipping & Delivery Policy

From Karachi to Gilgit — we deliver everywhere in Pakistan. Here's exactly how long it takes, what it costs and how to pay.

On this page
  1. Where we deliver
  2. Delivery time
  3. Delivery charges
  4. Order confirmation
  5. Payment methods
  6. Tracking your order
  7. Receiving your parcel

Where we deliver

We deliver nationwide — to all major cities, towns and most rural areas of Pakistan through trusted courier partners. Orders are dispatched from Lahore.

Delivery time

Orders are delivered within 2–4 working days after your order is confirmed.

  • We take and process orders 24/7 — every day of the week, including weekends and holidays.
  • Remote areas, bad weather, strikes or courier delays can occasionally add 1–3 days. We'll keep you updated if that happens.

Delivery charges

Order total below Rs 1,999Rs 199 delivery fee
Order total Rs 1,999 or moreFREE delivery
Cash on Delivery+Rs 99 COD fee on every COD order
Online payment (JazzCash / bank transfer)No COD fee — you save Rs 99

How the COD fee works: every Cash on Delivery order has a flat Rs 99 COD fee, whatever the order amount — even when delivery is free. Pay online by JazzCash or bank transfer instead and the COD fee is removed, so you save Rs 99.

The exact charges are always shown in your order summary before you place your order. Some bundle deals have their own city-based package price, shown on the product page.

Order confirmation

After you place an order, you'll see your Order ID (e.g. SN-12345) on screen. Our team will call or WhatsApp you to confirm your order and address before dispatch. Please keep your phone reachable — unconfirmed orders may be delayed or cancelled.

Payment methods

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): pay the rider in cash when your parcel arrives. A flat Rs 99 COD fee is added to every COD order.
  • Online payment: send payment via JazzCash or bank transfer (account details are shown at checkout) and WhatsApp us the screenshot. No COD fee — you save Rs 99.

Please only place Cash on Delivery orders you intend to receive. Refused parcels cost us two-way courier charges.

Tracking your order

Use our Track Order page with your Order ID or phone number, or message us on WhatsApp (0328 4430589) for an update.

Receiving your parcel

Please check that the outer packaging is intact when you receive your parcel. We strongly recommend recording a short unboxing video — it's required if you need a replacement for a damaged, faulty or wrong item (see our Returns & Replacement Policy).

Message us on WhatsApp — we're happy to help.
